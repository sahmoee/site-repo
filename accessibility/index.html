Accessibility

Built to be usable by everyone.

Updated September 29, 2026. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on this website and to support Apple’s accessibility features in our apps.

Website

  • Skip-to-content link, landmark regions and one h1 per page.
  • Full keyboard operation with a visible focus outline on every control; the mobile menu is a real button with an expanded state.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI outlines), with a stronger-contrast mode when your device requests it.
  • Meaningful images have alt text; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Animations stop if you enable Reduce Motion.

Apps

Apps are built with native SwiftUI controls and are designed for VoiceOver (labels on icon-only controls, decorative symbols hidden), Dynamic Type, Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, Dark Mode, Voice Control, and keyboard navigation on iPad and Mac (Full Keyboard Access and menu shortcuts). We keep improving; some older screens may fall short.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, email support@sowensstudios.com with the app or page and what happened. We respond within 5 business days and aim to fix reported barriers promptly.
