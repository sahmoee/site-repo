Privacy

Clear, useful,
and human.

Our products are designed to collect only what their features require and to keep sensitive work local when practical.

Effective August 14, 2026. This page explains the studio-wide approach. Product behavior can differ by enabled feature, account, and platform.

Website

This static website is delivered by Netlify through Cloudflare. If you submit an updates or support form, we receive the details you provide so we can respond or send the requested updates. We do not ask for passwords, keys, private media, journal content, or payment details through these forms.

Stocked

Kitchen, household, inventory, grocery, and recipe information is treated as private app data. Local storage is the foundation; optional synchronization is used for features such as household coordination and Mac-to-iOS recipe delivery.

Sesh

Session journals, personal notes, usage history, mood, and health-adjacent observations are sensitive. Core journaling is designed to work locally. Accounts and network services are used only for optional social, synchronization, music, push, and community features you enable.

Atlas

Guides and local history can remain on device. Document scanning or intelligent assistance may require platform or network processing depending on the feature selected; the app should explain that choice before data leaves the device.

Nova

Nova organizes source locations, metadata, profiles, and playback progress needed to present your personal library. It does not provide media. Network access is used to reach sources you configure and optional metadata services.

Reel

Imported screenshots, recordings, projects, previews, and exports are creative assets. They remain local unless you explicitly export or share them.

Orbyte

Storage scans, drive state, local projects, and network-resource settings are used to provide Mac management features. Orbyte is designed around local inspection and explicit actions.

Your choices

You can decline optional permissions, stop using network features, or contact support@sowensstudios.com to ask about a support or updates submission. Removing an app deletes local data according to Apple platform behavior unless it is also retained in an enabled sync service or backup.

Children and age-restricted products

Our general website is not directed to children. Sesh is an adults-only product intended for lawful use where cannabis is legal.

Changes

We update this notice when product behavior materially changes and show the effective date above.
