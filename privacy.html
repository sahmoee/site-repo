Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026 — SOwens Studios (sowensstudios.com)

Stocked stores your kitchen data — inventory, grocery lists, recipes, meal plans — on your device. If you sign in with Apple or join a household, data you choose to sync is stored with your iCloud account and, for shared households, on our sync service, used only to provide syncing.

AI features (receipt scanning, recipe import and generation) send only the content you submit to our server for processing; results are returned to your device. We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party trackers.

Optional diagnostics reports contain app version, device model, and item counts — never item names, recipes, or personal content.

To delete your data, use Settings → Data & Storage → Erase All Data in the app, which also removes iCloud backups. Questions: support@sowensstudios.com.
