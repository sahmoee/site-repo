Our website sets none, and our apps track nothing.

Effective September 29, 2026. This policy explains cookies and similar technologies on the Sowens Studios website and in our apps.

Our website

sowensstudios.com does not set cookies, does not use local storage for tracking, and loads no analytics, advertising, social-media or A/B-testing scripts. Fonts and images are served from our own domain. Because we use no non-essential cookies, there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent before setting anything that is not strictly necessary, and “Reject” will be as easy as “Accept”.

Our hosts, Netlify and Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security or load-balancing cookies when needed to deliver the site. These do not identify you for advertising.

Our apps

Our apps do not use advertising identifiers, do not include tracking SDKs, and do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because they do not track you.

  • Atlas video guides play through the YouTube player inside the app. YouTube (Google) may set cookies or use device storage in that player under Google’s cookie policy. You can avoid this by not playing videos.
  • Sign-in and connection pages (Spotify, Discord, Kroger, Trakt, Simkl, Real-Debrid and similar) open in Apple’s secure web session. Those companies set their own cookies on their own pages, governed by their policies. We cannot read them.
  • App storage. Apps store settings and content in on-device storage (files, databases and app preferences). This is not a cookie and is not shared for advertising. Deleting the app removes it.

Managing cookies

You can block or clear cookies in your browser or in Safari settings on your device. Blocking third-party cookies will not affect our website. Questions: support@sowensstudios.com.
