What it does

From recording to app preview.

These tools are part of current development. Testing and release availability are confirmed separately.

Build the story

Arrange, trim, split, and reorder clips on a visual timeline.

Frame the app

Place your screenshots or recordings inside device scenes and choose a background.

Add the details

Layer captions, music, transitions, and focus points around the moments that matter.

Export your way

Prepare portrait, landscape, square, GIF, and App Store sized outputs.

Good to know. AppPulse edits on device. Optional GitHub publishing needs your own configuration; the site does not host your projects.

Timeline control, transparent exports.

Current development · November 2026

Development includes validated clip trim, split and reorder actions; transparent ProRes exports; screenshot storyboards; and player lifecycle checks for paused and playable previews. The app edits local projects on iPhone and iPad.
