Moneyloom

Accounts, bills and plans. Together.

iPhone · iPadIn development
Get updatesSupport

What you can do.

A closer look at the current project.

Review what came in.

Import a bank statement, invoice, pay document or investment screenshot. Edit the extracted details before adding them to your records.

Give bills a place.

Track recurring bills, reminders, partial payments and payment history alongside account balances, debt and savings goals.

Share a household.

Create or join a household, approve membership and review conflicting edits. Keep local records available when the service is unreachable.

Control your connections.

Optional bank linking uses a separately configured provider. On-device document reading works without cloud review; cloud processing requires consent for each document.

Current work.

November 2026 development notes.

Account setup now starts with statement import. Household work focuses on membership approval, recoverable setup and explicit conflict review.
