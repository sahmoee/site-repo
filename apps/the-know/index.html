What you can do.

A closer look at the current project.

Start with your own story.

A blank caregiver journal organizes entries around Learning, Trying and Doing, with moments, notes and a local child profile.

Return to a thought.

Current journal work includes resumable drafts, observation dates, sorting and date filters so unfinished notes and older moments remain easy to find.

Choose what to share.

Prepare a selected caregiver handoff and review whether a profile is included. A handoff is a copy for a recipient, not automatic device synchronization.

Keep a recoverable record.

Versioned journal backups and reviewed additive import are part of current development, alongside safeguards for edits, deletion and retained recovery data.

Current work.

November 2026 development notes.

Journal tools are expanding alongside Daily Routine. The work includes saving unfinished drafts, selecting entries for caregiver handoffs and reviewing backup imports.
