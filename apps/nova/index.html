Bring your library together

Organize personal sources with metadata, posters, profiles, watch progress, history, favorites, and collections.

Discover your next watch

Browse user-installed catalogs, search, recommendations, airing information, people, and personalized shelves.

Play your way

Use supported SMB, direct URL, live TV, add-on, subtitle, download, and native playback options.

Keep progress portable

Use local storage, offline caches, iCloud configuration backup, and portable Nova backup snapshots.

Connect optional services

Add compatible metadata, subtitle, tracking, and source services with credentials protected in Keychain.

Fit every screen

Use adaptive iPhone and iPad layouts, a native Mac app and an artwork-led Apple TV library. Navigation, playback choices and available integrations vary by platform and build.

Nova does not provide media. You supply and remain responsible for your sources, permissions, and compliance with applicable law and service terms.
