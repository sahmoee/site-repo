The flagship app

Stocked.

Kitchen management and meal planning for people who cook. Keep your pantry, recipes, and grocery list working together instead of fighting each other.

🏠

Home

A daily brief that tells you what's expiring, what's ready to cook, and where your streak stands — the moment you open the app.

🧺

Inventory

Track everything in your pantry with expiry dates, barcode scanning, and receipt capture. Know what you have before you shop.

📖

Recipes

Browse by mood or cuisine, surface recipes you can make right now from what's on hand, and search in plain language.

🛒

Grocery List

Recipe-aware lists that sort themselves, with a Find in Store handoff to fifteen major grocery chains.

The studio

Small on purpose.

SOwens Studios is an independent shop focused on native Apple software. Every decision — product, design, and code — is made with care and a bias toward simplicity. The goal is software that feels like it was built by someone who actually uses it, because it was.

No growth-hacking, no dark patterns, no feature bloat. Just apps built to last.
