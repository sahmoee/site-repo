About the studio

Sowens Studios is an independent software studio operated by Jessie Owens. Its projects include Stocked for kitchen planning, Nova for personal media, Sesh for private session records, AppPulse for app previews, Let’s Date for nearby plans, and Atlas for guides.

Products

Stocked.

Inventory, groceries, recipes and guided cooking in one shared kitchen.

Nova

Bring your own media sources together with collections, watch progress and playback.

Sesh

Record sessions, follow stash changes, and look back at personal patterns.

AppPulse

Edit your screen recordings and screenshots into app previews and short videos.

Let’s Date

Find nearby places and events, save date plans, and keep private memories.

Atlas

Work through guides, save progress, and read documents with source context.
