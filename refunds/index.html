Refunds

Fair and simple.

Effective September 29, 2026. Most Sowens Studios apps are free to use. This page explains what happens if you buy something.

What is paid

Today the only Sowens Studios in-app purchase is the optional Household Sync upgrade in Stocked, if and when it is offered in your region. Its price is shown in your local currency in the purchase sheet before you confirm. There are no hidden fees, no auto-added extras and no purchases required to use the free features. No other Sowens Studios app charges you today. If that changes we will show the price and terms before you buy.

How to request a refund

Apple processes payment and refunds for App Store purchases, and we cannot issue them directly. To request one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose the purchase and select “Request a refund”. Apple decides each request under its own rules. If you have a problem the app should have solved, email support@sowensstudios.com with your app name and what happened and we will help fix it or support your refund request.

Cancelling

If a purchase ever becomes a subscription, you can cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access until the paid period ends.

Your legal rights

Depending on where you live, consumer law may give you a right to withdraw from a digital purchase or to a remedy if the product is faulty. Nothing on this page limits those rights. EU/UK customers: for digital content you can withdraw within 14 days unless you asked for immediate access and acknowledged losing that right; Apple handles the request.

Third-party services

Real-Debrid, Trakt, Simkl, Spotify and other services you connect have their own prices and refund rules. Contact them directly.
