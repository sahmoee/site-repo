Your data

Delete your data.

Three ways, from instant to server-side. No account or reason required.

Effective September 29, 2026. You can delete your data. Use the fastest option for your situation.

1. Delete data on your device (instant)

In each app open Settings → Legal & Data (Stocked and Stocked Mac: Settings; Nova: Privacy & Legal) and choose the erase option, or simply delete the app. This removes data stored on that device. It does not remove data in your iCloud account or data we hold on our servers.

2. Delete data in your iCloud

On iPhone open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → choose the app → Delete Data.

3. Ask us to delete data we hold on our servers

Use this if you used household sync, an account, tracking, sharing, community features or support. Tell us the app and the identifier shown in the app’s Settings → Legal & Data (if any). We will verify the request by replying to the email address you give, then delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email. Some records may be kept where required for security or law, and backups age out within 90 days.

We use your email only to verify and answer this request, and delete it afterwards except a minimal record that the request was completed. Prefer email? Write to support@sowensstudios.com.

Other rights

To access, correct or export your data, or to object to processing, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
